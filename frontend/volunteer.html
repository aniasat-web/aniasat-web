How to Volunteer

Our retreats rely entirely on volunteers. Here are the ways you can contribute.

Volunteer Roles

Kitchen Team

Help prepare meals, manage ingredients, and serve food during retreats. No prior cooking experience required.

Shopping & Procurement

Source ingredients from vendors, manage bulk orders, and coordinate deliveries for upcoming retreats.

Setup & Cleanup

Prepare spaces before retreats and help with cleanup afterward. Great for short-term volunteering.

Planning & Coordination

Help with menu planning, scheduling, and overall retreat logistics. Ideal for organized, detail-oriented volunteers.

What to Expect

  • Retreats typically run over a weekend (Friday evening through Sunday afternoon)
  • Volunteers can commit for as little as a single meal shift or the entire retreat
  • All meals are vegetarian and prepared with love by our kitchen team
  • Training and guidance are provided for all roles
  • Volunteers are welcome to participate in retreat activities when not on duty

Ready to Help?

We would love to hear from you! Reach out through our contact page to express your interest or ask questions.
