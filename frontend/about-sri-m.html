About Sri M

Sri M is the spiritual guide of Blossom Foundation, known for making timeless inner traditions practical and accessible for contemporary life.

Life Journey

1948

Born in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram), Kerala, on November 6, 1948.

Childhood & Youth

Experienced deep spiritual inclination early in life, including mastery of the Bhagavad Gita and formative meditative experiences.

Himalayan Training

At age nineteen, he met Maheshwarnath Babaji and trained under him for approximately three and a half years in the Himalayas.

Interfaith Learning

Later trained with Sufi masters, expanding his understanding across traditions while retaining a universal spiritual outlook.

Service & Impact

Social Initiatives

Sri M has led and inspired social welfare and ecological projects through the Satsang Foundation.

Walk of Hope

He initiated the 7,500 km Walk of Hope from Kanyakumari to Kashmir (January 12, 2015 to July 29, 2016) for peace, harmony, and environmental care.

Author

His books include Apprenticed to a Himalayan Master: A Yogi's Autobiography and The Journey Continues.

National Honor

He was conferred the Padma Bhushan in 2020 for distinguished service in spirituality and social work.

Context

The foundation's retreat ecosystem, including Sacred Grove, draws from this living stream of practice where inquiry, discipline, and compassionate action are integrated.
