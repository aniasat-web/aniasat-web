About Blossom Foundation

Blossom Foundation develops programs for spiritual and physical well-being and is building Sacred Grove as a center for yogic studies and the exploration of consciousness.

What We Do

We offer retreat-based and year-round learning programs rooted in authentic yogic traditions and practical inner work. These offerings are guided by Sri M and are designed for participants from all backgrounds.

Programs & Offerings

Kriya Yoga Retreats

Retreats that introduce the principles and practice process of Kriya Yoga, including yogic anatomy and core preparatory steps.

Upanishad Retreats

Deep study retreats where Sri M explores selected Upanishadic teachings with participants who come ready for inquiry and reflection.

Yoga Asana Classes

Weekly classes and annual courses rooted in the Hatha Yoga tradition, open to practitioners at different levels.

SamskritaM Swadhyaya

A structured Sanskrit learning initiative delivered in an accessible format for home-based study.
